# Get Trilorah onto the right screens

Start with the room. Decide which display faces the congregation, which one the speaker sees, and where any timer or stream output belongs.

In Trilorah, an output has a **job** and a **display**. The job determines its role. The display determines where its window appears. Check both before changing the layout or preparing more content.

## 1. Connect the hardware

Connect the projector or display, turn it on, and select the intended input. Confirm that the computer recognizes it.

Open **Dashboard → Outputs** in the desktop app and expand the tile. The display choices come from the computer's connected hardware. If the screen is missing, check the connection and operating-system display settings first.

Use the desktop app and the room’s hardware for this check.

## 2. Give each output its job

Choose the output's **job**: projector, stream, stage, or timer. Then use **display** to choose its destination.

A named display lets you make a deliberate assignment. **Automatic** assigns external screens in order. **No screen — off** closes and disables that output.

Read the resulting status. An output window on the laptop has not necessarily reached a dedicated external screen. Assignments can move or close open windows, so make them during setup.

## 3. Look at the destination

Prepare a recognizable test slide, such as “Projector check,” and send it live during rehearsal. Look at the physical display and check:

- The content appears on the intended screen.
- The image has no clipped edges.
- The words can be read from the back of the room.
- The displayed item matches what is live in Trilorah.

Repeat for each output you need. A laptop preview cannot tell you whether a projector has the correct input selected.

For a stage or timer output, check from the position of the person who will use it. Readable from the sound desk may still be too small from the lectern.

## 4. Adjust the presentation

Once the destinations are correct, use **Settings → Displays & output** for preferences such as stream layout, stage clock information, background fit, and background position.

Choosing the stream role creates a presentation output for that use. Your broadcasting setup or external streaming application still needs its own configuration.

## When something appears in the wrong place

Return to **Outputs** and compare the job and display with the room's connections. Recheck after reconnecting a cable; the previous arrangement may no longer describe the current hardware.

If the right display has the wrong material, inspect what is live. Preparing another item in preview does not send it to the output. If the content is correct but difficult to read, work through [the readability guide](12-make-projected-words-readable.md).

Finish by running [the first-service rehearsal](02-first-service-checklist.md) across the screens you have just assigned.
