# Research notes and article differentiation

Reviewed October 10, 2026. This is a small, current search-result and official-documentation review, not an SEO ranking study, keyword-volume report, customer survey, or reconstruction of competitors' launch histories.

## What informed the order

The strongest first-party evidence for this plan is the user's stated audience: church volunteers setting up and running services. The conversation also repeatedly asks for practical guidance about microphones, scripture, screens, media, and preparing a service. Those are direct needs; there are no support-ticket counts or customer conversion figures to attach to them.

The product source review provides a second constraint: recommend and explain implemented paths, and mark incomplete or unverified behavior honestly. The original map contains 59 task families; 59 tasks do not imply 59 blog posts.

Current official competitor documentation gives useful context for the kinds of answers a beginner can already find. It does not establish that Trilorah is better, that any sampled page ranks first, or that a competitor lacks a feature.

## Prioritization and evidence limits

The first six were chosen for dependency order and practical usefulness: understand the product, rehearse a complete service, establish audio, understand recognition, establish screens, and prepare the run. This is a qualitative product/adoption decision. Search volume, difficulty, and conversion potential have not been measured; they are intentionally not invented as numeric scores.

Before a future SEO expansion, review actual website query data and the questions raised by first-time volunteers. That would help decide whether to write another full tutorial, strengthen an existing page, or add a short help answer. Comparisons, pricing pages, customer case studies, and claims about time saved need evidence beyond this repository and were not fabricated for the launch calendar.

No outside brand monitoring, recurring searches, external posting, or outreach was created by this work.
