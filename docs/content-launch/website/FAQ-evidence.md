# FAQ editorial and evidence record

Reviewed October 17, 2026. This is an internal companion to [FAQ.draft.md](FAQ.draft.md). The draft contains 12 main-site answers and five download answers; the identifiers in its HTML comments let an implementation reuse the same answer without duplicating a second manuscript.

## Publication state

These answers describe inspected working-tree behaviour. They have not been verified against downloaded installers, a real soundboard/projector, the distributed speech models or a production cloud account. No application, deployment, account, database policy or release asset was changed or tested for this draft. No credentials, private files or production records were read.

On October 17, a browser fetch of the [configured v0.4.8 release](https://github.com/tomiwadoesux/trilorah2/releases/tag/v0.4.8) returned a fetch/cache error. That does not prove the release is absent or private, but it leaves public availability and installer contents unverified here. [release.ts](../../../web/src/app/download/release.ts) names Mac arm64 and Windows x64 installers, and [electron-builder.yml](../../../electron-builder.yml) configures those architectures; do not infer Intel Mac, Windows ARM, Linux support or minimum hardware/OS requirements from those names.

The founder confirmed that **support@trilorah.com and privacy@trilorah.com are not active yet**. They remain proposed addresses. The FAQ does not present either as a working contact. Confirm activation and monitoring before adding a contact/footer action, and link it to the [support draft](../../support/SUPPORT-PAGE.draft.md) once that page and inbox are ready.

## Scope and pricing decision

The broad question “What does Trilorah cost?” cannot receive a complete public answer until the current download/access terms and any offered services are settled. The founder explicitly deferred the paid offering. Do not invent a free plan, church-size price boundary, provider allowance, paid support entitlement, subscription or refund term to fill that gap.

**F03** instead answers the implemented setup question about separate provider access and costs. It does not say that the desktop app is a paid download or a free commercial offering. Before a pricing page exists, show only confirmed access terms beside the download button; keep the unresolved business decision here. A source-code licence is not a promise of hosted service pricing.
