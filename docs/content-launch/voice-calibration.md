# Trilorah humour calibration

Updated 11 October 2026. This records the founder's feedback and candidate directions. The founder has since asked us to rebuild the existing writing and prepare a longer-term brand plan. That authorizes drafting in this direction; it does not make every sample an individually approved line. Read this after the two brand studies: this later feedback overrides their exploratory tone samples. The working reference for the rebuild is [TONE.md](TONE.md).

## What the founder has told us

The preferred direction is bold, cheeky humour delivered with a serious face. The founder explicitly wants short ad-libs: a parenthetical comment that adds a second comic beat. Duolingo is a reference for personality and mischief, not a request to copy its mascot or campaigns.

Preferred starting point: “Your phone has been promoted.” The founder suggested additions such as “(you should thank me)” and “(literally)”. This is direction for exploration, not approval of every variant. In particular, “literally” should earn its place through an actual double meaning.

Rejected: “Sunday has declined to move.” The founder found it excessively AI-like. Also rejected: “The preacher has taken a detour.” The founder described it as AI trying to be human. Do not generate more calendar, desk or booth personification and assume it solves the problem.

Other samples from the prior round have no individual approval. Silence is not approval.

## Working interpretation to test

Start with a recognisable product or church situation. Find a specific observation, then add a short aside that changes the meaning, admits something or lets Trilorah take a little too much credit. The aside must relate to that sentence. A generic “you're welcome” after every feature would quickly become another template.

Use ordinary spoken words. Leave room between jokes. Deliver the practical explanation clearly after the opening. The brand can speak as “we”; a visibly speaking companion can use “I”. Keep action labels literal so a volunteer understands what will happen.

Candidate samples, awaiting feedback:

- Mobile introduction: “Your phone has joined the media team. (It was here every Sunday anyway.)”
- Mobile campaign: “Finally, a reason to be on your phone in church. (An official one.)”
- Companion, after locating the requested control: “Here's the button. (I do work here.)”
- A variation on the founder's preferred line: “Your phone has been promoted. (We put in a good word.)”

These are independent choices, not four lines to stack in one introduction. Mobile copy requires the same-network, connected-desktop explanation nearby. Companion copy must correspond to an action it actually completed.

## Research that changes the approach

[Duolingo's Super Bowl campaign account](https://blog.duolingo.com/super-bowl-commercial-2024/) describes using audience reactions to choose the creative premise. Its brief asides respond to concrete details in the story. The transferable lesson is to observe a real audience situation and test the response; borrowed eccentricity is insufficient.

[Duolingo's explanation of silly sentences](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-silly-sentences-can-help-you-learn/) connects playful writing to the work the lesson needs to do. For Trilorah, the feature fact and intended action come first in the writing process.

[Duolingo's copy-testing account](https://blog.duolingo.com/copy-testing-experiments/) describes comparing alternative messages and finding that results can differ across languages. Founder approval establishes the proposed voice; volunteer feedback still matters when the copy meets its audience.

The old official writing/voice and writing/tone URLs redirected to the design blog during this research. Do not present their search-index excerpts as a current live policy.

## How to improve the next round

Keep the approved and rejected lines with the founder's actual reasons. Change one variable at a time: premise, aside, or degree of cheekiness. Present three or four strong options rather than a long list of filler. Read each aloud and remove any words needed only to explain the joke. Test a winning pattern on a second real product situation before calling it a reusable voice rule.

This process improves the instructions and examples supplied to future writing work. It does not retrain the underlying model. The founder's later request to rebuild the writing allows full drafting to proceed; publication and release checks remain separate.
