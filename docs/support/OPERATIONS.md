# Trilorah support setup and handling

Internal operating draft, October 17, 2026. The [support page](SUPPORT-PAGE.draft.md) is written; the contacts and handling process must be put into operation before publishing it.

## Set up two addresses

Use `support@trilorah.com` for product questions and private security reports, and `privacy@trilorah.com` for data questions and requests. The founder has access to five domain addresses but does not need to use all five. Both can route into one monitored inbox if supported by the mail provider.

The founder confirmed on October 17, 2026 that neither address is active yet. Keep both marked as proposed until activation and testing are complete.

Confirm the actual inbox/alias setup, test receiving and replying from each address, and assign a responsible person. Store access securely and give it only to the people handling requests. No addresses, aliases, ticketing tools or email automation were created by this documentation work. No support hours or response-time promise has been established.

## Handle a product report

1. Record the app version, OS, observed result, expected result, reproduction steps and any error. Ask only for the missing details needed to investigate.
2. Try a rehearsal example in an appropriate development/test environment. Keep live church accounts, displays and real material out of a reproduction unless separately authorised and necessary.
3. Distinguish installation, audio input, transcription, recognition, projection, network, account and import problems; they have different next checks.
4. Provide a verified recovery step or identify what remains unknown. Link the existing guide and correct it if the report exposes a stale instruction.
5. Record the resolution and any product change needed. Do not turn a single report into a claim about general reliability or customer success.

Useful first reply:

> Thanks for the details. To check this, we need your Trilorah version, operating system and the exact steps before the problem appeared. If there was an error message, please include its wording. Please leave out API keys, passwords and private service material.

## Handle a privacy request

Establish the applicable request process with the operator and legal reviewer before promising deadlines or rights for every jurisdiction. The Privacy Policy and this process must agree.

- Record receipt and the request type with minimal necessary information. Identify the account/service and whether the request concerns Trilorah-operated storage, a church's local computer or another provider.
- Use appropriate identity/authority verification. Do not ask for a password or government ID as the routine first step.
- Trace local, cloud, queued, backup and provider copies that are relevant. Archiving a service, ending sharing, removing local data or signing out are not automatically erasure of every copy.
- Make only changes the requester is entitled and authorised to request. Explain the actual outcome and any applicable retention, technical or legal exception.
- Follow required response and appeal procedures where applicable. Do not claim completion from an unverified delete button or assume that a provider's settings implement the operator's chosen retention schedule.

Useful initial reply, once the inbox is monitored:

> We received your data request. Please tell us which account or service it concerns and what you would like us to review. Do not send your password. We may need to verify that you are authorised to make the request before taking action.

## Handle a security report

Keep the initial report private, preserve the relevant evidence and identify the affected code or deployment. Avoid requesting or forwarding active secrets. Route potential exposure of personal data to the operator for assessment of any necessary response. Do not promise a bounty, a secure-upload service or a response SLA that has not been established.
