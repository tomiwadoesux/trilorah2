# Project instructions

## Product writing and brand

When creating or changing reader-facing website, app, guide, email or marketing copy:

- Read [.agents/product-marketing.md](.agents/product-marketing.md) for the audience, positioning, voice references and product-evidence rules.
- Use [Trilorah's tone guide](docs/content-launch/TONE.md) as the shared voice reference. Use the [website writing briefs](docs/content-launch/WEBSITE-WRITING.md) to write page copy during implementation; complete page manuscripts are not required in advance.
- Check current implementation and release availability for capability claims. Existing articles and the product map can be stale; they are starting points, not proof of current behaviour.
- Update the relevant help, brief and product-map lesson when changing the behaviour they describe. Keep unresolved business or legal decisions in internal notes rather than inventing them in public copy.

## Final UI review

Before the final push, use the project's [UI Design Checklist](.agents/skills/sn-ui-checklist/SKILL.md) to review the app and report the highest-priority findings.
